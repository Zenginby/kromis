Cookie Notice

What Kromis Studio stores in your browser — and what it does not.

1. In short

We use only two cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to work. There are no third-party cookies, no tracking (analytics) cookies and no advertising cookies. That is why we do not ask for cookie consent: strictly necessary cookies are exempt (ePrivacy Directive art. 5(3); the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board's cookie guidance draws the same line).

2. The cookies we use

CookiePurposeLifetimeFlags
kromis_oturumYour session: tells the server you are signed in. The cookie holds a random token; the server stores only a hash of it.30 days, extended on each use (sliding); deleted when you sign outSecure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax
kromis_langYour interface language choice (Settings → Language); the page is rendered on the server in that language.1 yearSecure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax

Secure: the cookie travels only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. HttpOnly: scripts on the page cannot read it. SameSite=Lax: it is not sent with requests originating from other sites.

3. Browser storage (localStorage)

Some interface preferences (selected model, aspect ratio, last opened panel and the like) are kept in your browser's own storage. This data is not sent to the server; it stays in that browser and is removed when you clear your browser data. Preferences tied to your account are stored on the server with your account (see the Privacy Notice).

4. What we do not use

  • No analytics or tracking cookies (Google Analytics and the like).
  • No advertising networks, social media plug-ins or fingerprinting.
  • The checkout page opens on Polar's own domain; Polar's own cookies live on that page under Polar's cookie policy — they are not loaded on our pages.
  • Error monitoring (Sentry) runs server-side; it loads no script into your browser and receives no personal data (request bodies, e-mail, prompts).

5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If kromis_oturum is blocked you cannot sign in; if kromis_lang is blocked the language is determined again on each visit from your browser language or your account.

6. Changes

If we add a new cookie or storage mechanism this notice is updated and its version number advances; if a type requiring consent (tracking, advertising) were ever added we would ask for your consent separately.
