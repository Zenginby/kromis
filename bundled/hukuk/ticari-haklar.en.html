1. Kromis claims no rights

Kromis Studio claims no ownership or licence rights in the media you generate. You do not grant Kromis permission to use your output for advertising or promotion either; your content is processed only to provide the service to you. Whether AI-generated output enjoys copyright protection varies from country to country, and Kromis cannot grant a right or give a guarantee on that question.

2. Paid plans: commercial use

On the Basic and Pro plans you may use the images and videos you generate for personal or commercial purposes — sell them, publish them, use them in your products and client work — to the extent the licence of the model's provider allows. No watermark is added.

The extent is defined by the providers' rules, which are binding; Kromis cannot extend them:

  • OpenAI / Azure OpenAI models (GPT Image family): the OpenAI Usage Policies and the Azure OpenAI service terms.
  • Google models (Gemini, Imagen, Veo): the Google Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.
  • Black Forest Labs (FLUX family; via fal.ai or Azure): the BFL licence and terms.
  • Other models offered through fal.ai: the fal.ai terms of service and the respective model's own licence (the model catalogue shows each model's name and provider).

If a provider prohibits a particular use (for example using a model's output to train a competing model), that prohibition applies to you as well. When you generate with your own key (BYOK), your own provider agreement governs.

3. Free plan: personal use and watermark

Images generated on the Free plan are for personal, non-commercial use only and carry a Kromis watermark. Cropping, covering, erasing or otherwise removing the watermark is prohibited; commercial use of an image whose watermark has been removed is a breach of these terms. Video generation is disabled on the Free plan.

When you move to a paid plan, new generations come without a watermark; images generated earlier on the Free plan keep their watermark.

  • When you publish output, complying with any AI-content labelling obligations of your country (for example Article 50 of the EU AI Act) is your responsibility.

The Kromis Studio name and logo are outside the scope of these rights: saying "made with Kromis" in your products is fine, but you may not use the name or the logo as the brand of your own product.
